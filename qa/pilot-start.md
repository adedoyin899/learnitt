## 3. Screen-by-Screen Findings

### 3.1 Daily Learning Loop (`/today` · Screens S01–S04)
- **Session Card:** Displays today's scheduled topic ("SQL Fundamentals"), estimated time (60m), and MVD task (15m).
- **Check-in Drawer:** Opens smoothly on mobile tap and desktop click. Allows 1-tap outcome selection (`done`, `mvd_only`, `partial`, `missed`).
- **Perceived Save:** Local optimistic save completes in **< 40ms** before background sync to Supabase.
- **Offline Behavior:** Disconnecting network triggers offline status badge. Today schedule and active goal load from Dexie IndexedDB cache under 1 second. Offline check-in is saved into the Outbox and automatically synced upon reconnection.

### 3.2 Plan Engine & Timeline (`/plan` · Screen S05)
- **Feasibility:** Dynamic calculation displays projected completion date and weekly hour load.
- **Schedule Adaptation:** When study time falls behind, the adapt button opens schedule remedies (reducing session lengths or extending milestones) with one-click application.
- **Responsive Layout:** Timeline collapses cleanly from multi-column grid to vertical card stack at 360px without horizontal scrollbars.

### 3.3 Study Materials & Extraction (`/materials` · Screens S07–S09)
- **Upload Dropzone:** Accepts PDF and text files; enforces magic-byte security checks, blocks renamed `.exe` files, and prevents SSRF attacks.
- **Passage Retrieval:** Full-text chunk search scopes queries strictly to the authenticated user's documents.
- **Consent Gate:** AI tutor and quiz routes strictly enforce `profiles.ai_materials_consent` before querying material passages.

### 3.4 AI Tutor & Journal (`/ask` & `/journal` · Screens S10 & S11)
- **Citations:** Every AI explanation cites verified chunk passages with visual confidence badges. Responses without sources are explicitly labelled.
- **Journal History:** Supports weekly plain-language reflection summaries generated by pure deterministic logic from check-in data.

### 3.5 Quizzes & Rubric Grading (`/quizzes/[id]` · Screens S12 & S13)
- **Recall & Applied Questions:** Recall multiple choice options provide immediate feedback.
- **User Override:** If an AI evaluation grades harshly, the user can override the score with a single tap, recording the override reason and resetting skill evidence accurately.

### 3.6 Spaced Review Queue (`/review` · Screen S15)
- **Empty State:** When no reviews are due, displays clear illustration and message: *"Nothing due today."* with quick action links to Today or Progress.
- **Review Items:** Spaced intervals increment upon passing (1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days) and reset to 1 day on failure. Review block capped at 15 minutes.

### 3.7 Settings & Data Controls (`/settings` · Screen S16)
- **Reminder Configuration:** Selects for daily time, timezone, and weekday chips.
- **Delivery Channels:** Verified switches for Email notifications (Resend) and Web Push (VAPID). Includes honest iOS note: *"Push works on iPhone only when Learnitt is added to your home screen. Email is the backup."*
- **Account Controls (Epic I):** One-click data export as JSON + ZIP bundle (`POST /export`). Typed confirmation for Goal Deletion and Account Erasure (`DELETE /account`).

### 3.8 Pilot Metrics Dashboard (`/metrics` · PRD Section 18)
- Displays all 7 pilot metrics in real time:
  1. Minimum Viable Day (MVD) Rate (Target: ≥ 80%)
  2. Weekly Plan Adherence (Target: ≥ 70%)
  3. Check-in Friction Time (Target: < 30s)
  4. Verified Skills Count & Growth
  5. Evidence Mix Share E2/E3 (Target: ≥ 60%)
  6. AI Calls / Day & Cap Adherence (Target: ≤ 30 in 95% of days)
  7. Evaluator Health & Grader Override Rate (Target: < 25%)
- Generates and exports the structured one-page Monthly Review markdown template (`GET /api/v1/metrics?format=markdown`).
